PARALLEL INDEX

EQUAL SPACING: parallel pairs on both sides of own ship. Bearing is true; spacing is perpendicular distance.

INDIVIDUAL LINES (select above): up to 3 lines. Bearing = true direction of the line (normally your planned track). Distance = perpendicular distance from own ship. INDEX = the line a fixed echo should slide along. LIMIT = the closest you accept (red dashed).
