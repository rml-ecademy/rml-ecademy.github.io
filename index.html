Packages

Each package opens the decks for its rank and every rank below it. Pay once, log in with your email, and the simulators open on this site.

Written by a Master Mariner, for the officers coming up behind.

ECADEMY is built and kept up to date by a serving ship's captain. Every trainer follows how the work is done on board: the checks a Third Officer makes before a PSC inspection, the plot a Second Officer keeps on a busy crossing, the draft survey a Chief Officer signs.

New trainers are added to each deck as they are finished. All Access members receive them as part of their package.

Capt. ReyMaster Mariner, founder of ECADEMY

ECADEMY is a training aid. It does not replace STCW-approved courses, type-specific training or your company's procedures, and it must not be used for real navigation.
