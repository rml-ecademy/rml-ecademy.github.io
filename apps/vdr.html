KEY GUIDE

ACK silences the buzzer (ERROR LED stays on until the fault is cleared) · TEST lamp & buzzer test (888) · DIMMER − / + brightness · INCIDENT SAVE: lift the cover, then PUSH and HOLD until the SAVE LED starts blinking (≈3 s). With several errors the display cycles through the codes.
