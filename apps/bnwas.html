PANEL GUIDE (this training unit)

Key switch (Master): OFF · ON (always active) · AUTO (active only when heading/track control is engaged). Click the left / right half of the lock to turn it.
RESET (flashes in stage 1): restarts the dormant period. Hold RESET 3 s = EMERGENCY CALL (stage 2 + 3 at once).
SET/TEST: press = lamp & buzzer test (888). Hold 3 s (key in ON or AUTO) = settings: d dormant period 3–12 min, 3 stage-3 delay 90–180 s; −/+ change, SET/TEST next, RESET save & exit.
− / +: dimmer. Display: MM:SS countdown of the dormant period while active (mode Aut / On shown for 2 s when the key is turned, OFF when off); during alarms it alternates AL1 / AL2 / AL3 with the time to the next stage; EC = emergency call.
