📻 HOW TO CHANGE THE RT CHANNEL

1. Press 1 RT/CH on the radio.
2. Turn the PUSH TO ENTER knob (mouse wheel, or click its left / right side) to scroll — or key in the channel / frequency with the number keys (e.g. 8 0 1 or 8 2 9 4).
3. Push the knob (click its centre) to set.
4. Press 0 TUNE to tune the antenna, then lift the handset and talk.
* = delete digit · # = J3E/H3E · CANCEL = back

CALLING ANOTHER SHIP / A COAST STATION (MF/HF)

Ship → ship: DSC individual call (ROUTINE) on 2177.0 kHz, propose an intership simplex working frequency (e.g. 4146, 6224, 8294 kHz). After the ACK (able to comply), set and TUNE that frequency and call by RT.
Ship → shore: DSC on 2189.5 kHz (MF) to the coast station, working on an ITU duplex channel (e.g. CH 801: ship TX 8195 / RX 8719 kHz).
RT call on 2182: keep it short — call, agree a working frequency, change. Never use 2187.5 / 8414.5 for routine calls.
Radio check reply: strength / readability 1–5 ("loud and clear" = 5/5).

RECEIVED DSC DISTRESS — WHAT TO DO

MF (2187.5): silence the alarm, do not acknowledge by DSC. Listen on 2182 kHz. If no coast station acknowledges and the ship is near you, acknowledge by RT on 2182 and inform the RCC.
HF: do not acknowledge by DSC. Listen on the RT/NBDP frequency of that band. If no coast station acknowledges within 5 minutes, send a DSC distress relay to the coast station / RCC — never to all ships.

FALSE DISTRESS ALERT — CANCEL

Do not switch off. Cancel by DSC distress cancellation (if fitted), then on the RT frequency of each band used (MF: 2182 kHz): "ALL STATIONS ×3, THIS IS name, call sign, MMSI, POSITION…, CANCEL MY DISTRESS ALERT OF date/time UTC = MASTER name, call sign, MMSI, date/time UTC." Log it in the GMDSS radio logbook.

WHAT IS REAL / SIMPLIFIED

Real: the frequencies, the hold-4-s distress button and steady light, the received-distress rules for MF and HF, the MAYDAY / acknowledgement / cancel formats, daily self test, DSC test call with a coast station, reserve battery checks.
