HOW TO (this training unit)

HAND → AUTO: steady the ship, mode selector to AUTO, press TAKE OVER — the autopilot keeps the present heading (HDG mode).
Course change: SET HDG → turn the knob to the new course (click left/right half = 1°, mouse wheel = 1°, Shift+wheel = 10°, or drag) → ACCEPT. The autopilot puts on rudder, the ship turns and the gyro repeater card rotates.
PRESET: choose turn limit — RUDDER limit or RATE OF TURN — knob to change, ACCEPT.
Mode selector: HAND · OFF (centre) · AUTO. OFF = bridge steering disconnected — emergency steering from the steering gear room with the local NFU buttons (hold PORT / STBD, release = rudder stays), orders by phone from the bridge.
AUTO → HAND: mode selector to HAND, steer with the helm (drag the wheel or buttons). Rudder order = wheel angle (follow-up).
⚙ brightness / palette · 🔇 silence alarm. The repeater has an outer 360° card and an inner fine card (one turn = 10°).
