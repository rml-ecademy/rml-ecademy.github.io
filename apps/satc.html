DISTRESS ALERT — SAT-C

  1. Update the distress alert setup (F9 Distress): nature of distress, position, course, speed.
  2. Lift the cover, press and hold the DISTRESS button 4 s (it lights steady when sent).
  3. Wait for the distress alert acknowledgement from the LES / RCC.
  4. Follow with a distress priority message (F9) giving full details.
  5. Keep listening on VHF 16 / DSC and other GMDSS means.

FALSE DISTRESS ALERT — CANCEL

Do not switch off. Send a distress priority message to the RCC via the same LES: "CANCEL MY INMARSAT-C DISTRESS ALERT OF (date/time UTC) — ship name, call sign, IMN, position." Inform the Master and record it in the GMDSS radio logbook.

ROUTINE CHECKS (training)

Daily: terminal logged in, position updated (≤ 4 h), printer paper. Periodic: link test / PV test as required by the company and the equipment manual. Record all in the GMDSS radio logbook. Never test the distress button except in an approved test mode.
