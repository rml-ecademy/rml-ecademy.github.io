RECEIVED VHF DSC DISTRESS

Silence the alarm, do not acknowledge by DSC. Listen on CH16. If no coast station acknowledges and the ship in distress is in your vicinity, acknowledge by RT on CH16 and inform the coast station / RCC.

FALSE ALERT — CANCEL

Do not switch off. DSC distress cancellation (if fitted), then on CH16: "ALL STATIONS ×3, THIS IS name, call sign, MMSI, POSITION…, CANCEL MY DISTRESS ALERT OF date/time UTC = MASTER…". Log it.
